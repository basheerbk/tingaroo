What is inside every project

Everything arrives together, so nobody has to piece a lesson together the night before.

  • Step-by-step tutorial

    Every step with the exact blocks to drag, shown as pictures.

  • Finished project file

    Open it to see the goal, or to check a stuck student's work.

  • Parent helper page

    Time, age, what you need, and what to say when a child gets stuck.

  • Lesson plan

    Goals, timing, a warm-up, the main activity and a wrap-up.

  • Worksheet and challenge cards

    Prompts for every student, plus extras for fast finishers.

  • Rubric and answer key

    Grade a project by checking what it must contain.

Questions

What is Scratch?

A free coding language where kids snap blocks together to make games and animations. There is no typing of code.

Do we need to buy any software?

No. Scratch and TurboWarp are free and run in a web browser. The physics games use the Physics extension in TurboWarp.

Which ages are the projects for?

Grades 3 to 8, about ages 8 to 13. Every project shows its grade band and how long it takes.

Is Tingaroo part of Scratch?

No. Tingaroo is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Scratch Team, MIT or TurboWarp.

Who makes Tingaroo?

Basheer, a maker-educator who teaches kids to build with code and electronics. Find more of his work at basheer.diy.
